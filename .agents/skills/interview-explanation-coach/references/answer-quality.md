# Answer quality rubric

Rate each dimension 0–2 and explain the most valuable improvement.

- Directness: answers the exact question before adding context.
- Accuracy: technically sound without grand claims.
- Plain language: understandable without buzzword substitution.
- System position: says where the concept or work fits.
- Trade-off: names a cost, boundary, or alternative.
- Evidence: connects to a real example at the correct experience level.
- Ownership clarity: separates personal work, team work, and unknowns.
- Natural delivery: sounds like Mali, not a generated essay.

Do not optimize for length alone. A succinct answer is one that contains no unnecessary material while preserving the idea the interviewer needs.
