# Interviewer lenses

- Recruiter/HR: eligibility, motivation, communication, stability, compensation, and process risk. Ask which criteria are truly mandatory, who owns the role, and what the process tests.
- Technical lead: reasoning, debugging, code quality, system learning, review response, and safe change. Ask about architecture, failure modes, code review, and first technical responsibilities.
- Engineering manager: delivery, teamwork, growth, prioritization, reliability, and support needs. Ask what success and mentorship look like after three months.
- Founder/owner: customer outcome, judgement, adaptability, cost, speed, and mission. Ask what must become true for the company to win and how the role affects it.
- Product/designer: user understanding, ambiguity, interaction quality, evidence, and iteration. Ask how problems are discovered and decisions validated.
- Future teammate: day-to-day clarity, dependability, curiosity, conflict, and collaboration. Ask what surprises newcomers and what makes someone effective on the team.

The interviewer may occupy several lenses. Use their questions and follow-ups as evidence; do not force a label.
