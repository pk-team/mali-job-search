# Search lanes

Use the lanes that fit the request and report which ones were searched.

1. Job boards: useful for demand language and specific openings, even when the application route is poor.
2. Direct employer career pages: verify the employer and find less mediated routes.
3. Technical leaders and public engineering work: talks, blogs, repositories, communities, and product releases.
4. Small product companies and agencies with visible craft: assess client work, team, review practice, and direct contact separately.
5. Internships, apprenticeships, returnships, and trainee programmes: verify eligibility and whether learning is structured.
6. Developer and design communities: recurring meetups, study groups, project nights, hackathons, open source, and lightning talks.
7. Contribution routes: documentation, design systems, frontend fixes, community websites, and bounded volunteer work with clear ownership and safety.
8. Warm graph: friends, former collaborators, organizers, maintainers, and people who have directly seen Mali's work.

Avoid unpaid speculative work for companies disguised as an interview. A bounded public learning exercise or open-source contribution should benefit Mali regardless of hiring outcome.
