# Route types

Do not combine these categories:

1. US-based employee, including “remote US”: requires US work authorization and an employer-approved state/location.
2. Globally distributed employee: employer has a local entity or supported employment arrangement in Thailand.
3. Employer of record: a third party legally employs the worker locally for the foreign company.
4. International contractor: services supplied from Thailand; different protections, tax, payment, termination, and intellectual-property terms.
5. Agency/outsourcing placement: the worker may serve a foreign client while employed or contracted by an intermediary.
6. Relocation and visa sponsorship: role and candidate must satisfy immigration and employer sponsorship constraints.
7. Open-source/community route: contribution creates evidence, relationships, and sometimes employment without promising either.
8. Paid trial, fellowship, apprenticeship, or fixed project: verify payment, scope, ownership, mentorship, and whether it is genuine work or speculative free labor.

For every “remote” role, answer: remote from where, under what relationship, at what level, with what timezone, and with what proof threshold?
