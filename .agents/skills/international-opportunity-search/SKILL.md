# International opportunity search

Create a current, dated map of routes that are actually available from Thailand and the evidence threshold each requires.

Read `evidence/mali-baseline.html`, `paths/index.html`, and [references/route-types.md](references/route-types.md). Research live employer and government sources for every current claim.

## Research rules

- Separate occupation-level norms from an individual employer's requirements.
- Distinguish no-degree eligibility from competitiveness. “Degree not listed” does not mean junior-friendly.
- Verify location scope: US-only, named countries, timezone region, worldwide, relocation, or unknown.
- Distinguish employee, local entity, employer of record, agency, and independent contractor arrangements.
- Treat visa, tax, worker classification, and employment-law conclusions as professional-advice areas. State the apparent route and what must be verified; do not improvise legal advice.
- Prefer direct company listings, hiring-process pages, public handbooks, and official government sources over remote-job aggregators.
- Record role level, evidence expectations, communication model, trial/work sample, salary location policy when public, and whether Thailand is explicitly supported.
- Do not apply, contact, register, or disclose Mali's personal information without explicit authorization.

## Output

For each route, report: current evidence, geographic/legal gate, degree gate, experience gate, what employers visibly value, Mali's present evidence, missing proof, option value, downside, and next low-cost experiment.

Update `paths/index.html` when findings are durable and cite the checked date. Do not add a current job to `jobs/index.html` unless it is a specific plausible opportunity worth a full dossier.
