Lifecycle

  1. Captured. Source, access date, employer, title, location, and durable summary saved.
  2. Researched. Company and hiring route corroborated; uncertainty marked.
  3. Triaged. Pursue, network-first, learn-only, watch, or decline—with reasons.
  4. Practised. Glossary, project, and interview material completed to the chosen depth.
  5. Contacted. Application, introduction, event conversation, or direct outreach recorded.
  6. Learned. Outcome and new information update the heuristics.
