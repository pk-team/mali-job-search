Learn · build · explain · connect

A job search that produces an education.

Every real opportunity becomes a map of the company, the hiring route, the engineering ideas, a small project, and the explanations Mali needs to speak with confidence. An application is one possible output—not the only measure of progress.

The working thesis: Mali should not compete by pretending to have conventional credentials. She should make independent ability unusually easy to see: finished interfaces, clear system explanations, honest scope boundaries, thoughtful questions, and warm connections with people who can recognize the work.

The system

01

Research the opening

Save the facts, identify the real problem, estimate the hiring gate, and distinguish recruiter from employer.

 02

Deflate the buzzwords

Explain each term as an ordinary engineering choice: problem, trade-off, example, and boundary.

 03

Build one visible slice

Use modern .NET with React, SvelteKit, or Blazor where it clarifies the job's central ideas.

 04

Practice explaining

Prepare 15-second, one-minute, and deeper answers grounded in work Mali can actually demonstrate.

 05

Create human routes

Meetups, small communities, project demos, referrals, and direct technical conversations run in parallel.

 06

Grow the evidence

Record what is proven, adjacent, learning, or unknown. Never let ambition turn into an inflated claim.

What success looks like

The near-term goal is not “submit the most applications.” It is to create repeated, credible chances for a technical person to think: she has already done useful interface work, learns independently, understands the purpose of systems, and can explain her decisions.

Learning

She can explain a previously intimidating term and show it in a small working system.

Evidence

Each portfolio claim points to code, a design artifact, a commit, a screenshot, or a candid project note.

Access

More conversations occur with engineers, founders, designers, and community organizers—not only anonymous portals.

Agency

She chooses opportunities based on learning, support, and real work—not the prestige of a buzzword list.

Versioned working knowledge. Time-sensitive company and meetup research must show a checked date.
